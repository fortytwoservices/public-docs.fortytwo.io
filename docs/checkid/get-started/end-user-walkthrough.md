# End-user walkthrough

This is what a new employee sees the first time they set up their account with CheckID. It takes a few minutes on their phone, and nobody in IT has to hand out a password. The logo, colours, fonts and text are all yours to customise, see [Custom branding](../customise/custom-branding.md).

<video class="demo-video" controls preload="metadata" playsinline src="../media/guides/CheckID_Clicked_demo_june2025.mp4"></video>

:::::screens
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-1.webp" title="Verify your identity"}
The employee opens your CheckID page and signs in with an ID they already use, such as MitID, Vipps, BankID or ID-porten.
::::
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-2.webp" title="Choose what to do"}
They start the setup as a new employee, or recover an account they already have.
::::
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-3.webp" title="Pick the account"}
If the person has more than one account, they choose the one to set up.
::::
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-4.webp" title="Install Microsoft Authenticator"}
CheckID links to the app in the right store, and asks them to come back once it is installed.
::::
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-5.webp" title="Add the account"}
CheckID shows the username and a Temporary Access Pass, and walks them through adding the account in Authenticator.
::::
::::screen{src="../media/guides/step-6.webp" title="All set"}
The account is ready. Helpful links point them to what comes next.
::::
:::::

Want to see what happens behind the scenes at each step? Read [How the flow works](./how-the-flow-works.md).
