# Home

The control panel opens on **Home**. The first line tells you how things are, for example *1 thing needs you, Kari.*, or that everything is running.

![The control panel home page](/screenshots/ai-universe/home.webp)

## What it shows

| Section | What you see |
|-|-|
| **At a glance** | Apps (production and sandbox), agents across every app, AI activity over the last 14 days and spend over the last 30. |
| **Needs attention** | What is waiting for you, such as a new platform version with **Open Upgrade**. |
| **Your apps** | Your apps, filtered by *Needs attention*, *Production* or *All*, with **See all** for the full list. |

From the top you can also **Create an app**, or **Invite a builder** to let a colleague in who will create apps.

## The tabs

Administrators see these tabs across the top:

| Tab | What it is for |
|-|-|
| **Home** | This page. |
| **Apps** | Every app, in a list or as cards, with filters by status, owner and department. See [Create an app](../apps/create-an-app.md). |
| **AI** | Models, safety rules and pausing all AI. See [AI and models](ai.md). |
| **Wisdom** | Rules for AI coding assistants. See [Wisdom](../apps/wisdom.md). |
| **People** | Who can sign in to the control panel. See [People](people.md). |
| **Reporting** | **Cost**, **Compliance** and **Security**. See [Governance](../governance/security-and-data.md). |
| **Settings** | The platform's own settings. See [Settings](settings.md). |

People who are not administrators see their own apps and the help pages.

## Switching to an app

The switcher next to the logo, showing **Universe**, moves between the control panel and the apps you manage. In an app, the tabs change to that app's own: **Home**, **Agents**, **Wisdom**, **People**, **Build** and **Settings**.

![An app's Home tab](/screenshots/ai-universe/app-home.webp)

:::tip[Dark mode]
Everything works in dark mode too. Switch under **Appearance** in the account menu.

![The control panel in dark mode](/screenshots/ai-universe/home-dark.webp)
:::
