# People in the control panel

The **People** tab decides who can sign in to the control panel and what they may do there.

![The People tab](/screenshots/ai-universe/people.webp)

:::info[This is not access to apps]
Letting someone into the control panel does not give them access to any app. Each app has its own list, on that app's [People](../apps/people.md) page.
:::

## Let someone in

1. Under **Let someone in**, find a person or a group in your directory.
2. Choose what they may do here.
3. Confirm. They can sign in from their next visit.

## Who can create apps

**Who can create apps** decides whether creating an app needs a special role. When it does, only people you give the creator role can create a new app. Administrators (*Universe admin*) always can.

**Invite a builder** on the home page brings you here, to let in a colleague who will create apps.

## Remove someone

Choose **Remove**. The person loses the role straight away, but anyone already signed in keeps what they have until their sign-in expires. Every change is recorded, and you can grant access again later.
