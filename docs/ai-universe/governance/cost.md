# Cost

Open **Reporting** and choose **Cost** to see *what AI is costing you, where it goes, and what it was spent on*.

![Reporting, Cost](/screenshots/ai-universe/reporting-cost.webp)

## What it shows

| Figure | What it means |
|-|-|
| **Spent on AI in the last 30 days** | Total model spend across every app. |
| **Spent on AI in the last day** | Yesterday's spend, with the tokens used. |
| **Spend per day** | A chart from the daily roll-up, priced on the day. |
| **Where it went** | Apps ranked by spend over the same days. Spend that is not tied to an app is shown on its own row. |

Usage is collected by the AI gateway, so it covers every app without the apps having to report anything.

:::note
The chart starts filling in the day after the platform is installed, when the first day has been rolled up. Usage on a model without a price is left out of the totals, and the page says so.
:::

## Keeping cost down

- Use **Quick** or **Balanced** on agents that do not need deep reasoning. See [Agents](../apps/agents.md#brief-it).
- Offer cheaper models for simple work on the [AI](../control-panel/ai.md) page.
- Check **Where it went** for apps that grow faster than expected.
