# AI Universe

AI Universe gives your organisation one place to build, run and govern AI apps. Each team gets apps that fit its work, such as an HR assistant, a contract reviewer or a sales researcher. IT keeps control of the models, the data, the people and the cost.

Everything runs in **your own Azure subscription and Entra tenant**. Fortytwo has no access to it. Your data, your prompts and your models never leave your cloud.

## Who it is for

| You are | You use AI Universe to |
|-|-|
| **A business team** | Ask for an app, brief its agent in plain words, and invite colleagues. No code needed to get started. |
| **A developer** | Build on the app's code in the browser with Studio, or in your own coding tool. Sign-in, the AI gateway and hosting are already handled. |
| **IT and the platform owner** | Choose the models, set the safety rules, manage who may create apps, follow the cost and keep the platform up to date. |
