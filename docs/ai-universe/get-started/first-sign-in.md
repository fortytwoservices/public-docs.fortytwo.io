# First sign-in

Open the control panel address from the installer and sign in with your work account. The person who ran the installer can always sign in. Everyone else needs to be let in first, see [People in the control panel](../control-panel/people.md).

## The setup guide

The first time you sign in, the setup guide greets you: *Let's get your platform ready.* Choose **Let's start**.

![The setup guide's welcome screen](/screenshots/ai-universe/setup-guide-welcome-dialog.webp)

The guide has three parts:

| Part | What it covers |
|-|-|
| **1. Get connected** | Create the GitHub App, install it on your organisation, turn on GitHub sign-in and link your own GitHub account. See [Connect GitHub](connect-github.md). |
| **2. Make it yours** (optional) | AI and data location, guardrails, branding and people. |
| **3. Start building** | Create your first app, or invite the people who will. |

When the required steps are done, the guide shows *You're connected. The rest is up to you.* Go through the optional steps one by one, or choose **Looks good, I'm done**.

![The final step of the setup guide](/screenshots/ai-universe/setup-guide-ready-dialog.webp)

If you chose **I'll do this later**, the home page keeps a **Finish platform setup** card until the required steps are done. You can also open the guide at any time from **Setup guide** in the account menu.

![Home before setup is finished, with the Finish platform setup card](/screenshots/ai-universe/home-finish-platform-setup.webp)

## The account menu

Your initials in the top right corner open the account menu.

![The account menu](/screenshots/ai-universe/account-menu.webp)

| Item | What it does |
|-|-|
| **Your profile** | Your own details, and the GitHub account linked to you. |
| **Technical details** | Shows the parts IT needs on every page: resource names, identities and logs. Off by default. |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark. |
| **Settings** | The platform's [settings](../control-panel/settings.md). |
| **Setup guide** | Opens the setup guide again. |

Under *Elsewhere in the portal* you also find **Documentation** and the **Designsystem**, which shows your platform's brand colours and components.
